# Netflix-Performance-Analysis-Dashboard
Built an Excel-based performance analysis dashboard for a subscription streaming platform, analyzing revenue trends, subscriber growth, churn rate, regional performance, and engagement (2023–2024). Used PivotTables, calculated fields, and slicers to generate actionable business insights and performance comparisons.

Key Insights (2024 vs 2023):

Revenue declined by 1.39% YoY, indicating slight stagnation rather than major decline.

New subscriptions decreased by 0.94%, suggesting slower acquisition in 2024.

Churn rate is 1.93%, with the Basic plan showing higher churn — indicating price-sensitive users.

Revenue performance was strongest in October & December, weakest in May & November.

Average watch time (166 mins) remains strong, indicating engagement is stable; revenue pressure likely driven by acquisition and churn, not content performance.
